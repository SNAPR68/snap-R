Founder / GTM Lead
15–20 hrs/week · All 13 weeks

  • DM outreach — 10 targeted messages per day
  • Live demos via Zoom for every interested lead
  • Personal onboarding email to every new signup within 24 hrs

Content Operator / VA
10–15 hrs/week · Weeks 3–13 · Part-time / Contractor

  • Repurpose demo recordings into short-form clips
  • Monitor and flag forum threads for founder response
  • Maintain outreach tracking spreadsheet
  • Compile weekly engagement report
Contractor rate $20–35/hr
Role 03 · Optional Multiplier
Real Estate Industry Advisor
2–4 hrs/week · Weeks 1–13 · Commission or Equity

  • Co-host for agent webinars (lends credibility)
  • Connects you to photographer networks and broker offices
Suggested comp $200/intro or 0.1–0.25% equity
Role 04 · Week 7+ Only
YouTube / Creator Partnership
1–2 hr setup + ongoing rev-share · Weeks 7–13
  • Kyle Handy, Jason Wardrop, Josh Cahill — 50K–300K RE agent subscribers each
  • Provide free Pro access + affiliate link (20–30% recurring commission)
  • Record a 10-min walkthrough video using your demo listing
  • No upfront fee — commission-only if structured correctly
Upfront cost $0 (rev-share only)
Time reality check: The founder role alone is 195–260 hours across 13 weeks. If you're also building product, plan for 60-hour weeks in Phase 1–2. The plan does not work at 2 hours/day. It requires 3–4 focused hours minimum on outreach alone, daily.

Strategic Decision

The Right Operating Model

Three models. Same strategy. Different leverage points. Choose based on your time availability.
Model A
Full Bootstrap
$2,340 total · 90 days · Founder-only
  • Lowest cash outlay — preserves runway
  • Forces founder to deeply understand the customer
  • No management overhead
  • All outreach is personally credible
  • Requires 20+ hrs/week from founder on GTM alone
  • Unsustainable if also building product
  • Phase 2 outreach volume will drop in Week 5–6
  • Slower to reach 100 — likely 100–120 days not 90
Model B
Lean Hire
$8,085 total · 90 days · Founder + Part-time VA
  • Founder focuses on demos + product; VA handles content and tracking
  • Outreach volume stays consistent through all 3 phases
  • Content repurposing turns one demo into 6 pieces of content
  • Hits 100 paying users in 90 days with high probability
  • ROI positive by month 3 even at Starter plan avg
  • Requires founder to hire and manage a contractor
  • VA needs 1–2 weeks to onboard and become effective
  • $8K requires confidence in the model before spending
Model C
Accelerated
$15,000–$20,000 total · 90 days · Founder + VA + Advisor + PR
  • Industry advisor opens association and broker doors immediately
  • PR placement in Inman or RIS Media in Week 6–8
  • Can target 200 users by Day 90 instead of 100
  • Positions for Series A narrative
  • Requires significant capital before product-market fit confirmed
  • PR placements are unpredictable ($3K–$8K with no guarantee)
  • Management overhead increases disproportionately
  • Overkill until Model B proves the channels work
Recommendation: Start with Model A for Weeks 1–2 (foundation — the work is founder-only regardless). Move to Model B at Week 3 by hiring a VA. This gives you 2 weeks to validate the outreach approach before committing the contractor budget. The $1,000/month VA spend should pay back in the first 3 conversions at Pro tier ($179/mo).

Final Recommendation
The Right Model: Start Bootstrap, Switch to Lean at Week 3
Weeks 1–2: Bootstrap (Model A). Every task is founder-only regardless — community research, demo prep, account warmup. Spend $107 on Canva, Zoom, and Product Hunt Ship. No other spend makes sense yet.

Week 3 trigger: If you have completed 100 DMs and gotten 5+ interested responses, hire the VA. That's your signal the channel works. Budget $1,000/month.

Week 7 trigger: If you have 30+ paying users, activate the YouTube creator program and Industry Advisor. Both are performance-based — no cash at risk until they convert.

What this is NOT: This is not a campaign. There is no "launch moment" that does the work. This is an execution system — 10 DMs/day, 5 forum answers/day, 1 demo/day minimum — running for 90 consecutive days. The budget is small because the leverage is founder credibility, not paid reach.

The real cost is time: 200 founder hours across 13 weeks. That is the actual investment. The $3,855 in Model B cash is the support structure around those hours — it does not replace them.
