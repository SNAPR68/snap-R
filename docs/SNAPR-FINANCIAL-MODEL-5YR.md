## 1. HOW SNAPR ACTUALLY CHARGES

SnapR is a **per-listing platform**. Not per-photo, not per-user-seat. An agent chooses:
1. **Plan**: Gold ($28 base) or Platinum ($30 base)
2. **Listing volume**: 5 to 300 listings/month (slider on pricing page)
3. **Billing cadence**: Pay-as-you-go, Monthly (40% off), or Annual (60% off)

The total bill = **price/listing x listings/month**.

### Actual Price Grid (from production code)

| Plan | Billing | 5-50 listings | 75-300 listings |
|------|---------|--------------|-----------------|
| **Gold** | Pay-as-you-go | $28/listing | $28/listing |
| **Gold** | Monthly | $20/listing | $16/listing |
| **Gold** | Annual | $16/listing | $11/listing |
| **Platinum** | Monthly | $22/listing | $18/listing |

**Examples of actual monthly bills:**
- Solo agent: Gold Monthly, 15 listings = **$300/mo**
- Small team: Gold Monthly, 50 listings = **$1,000/mo**
- Mid-size brokerage: Gold Annual, 100 listings = **$1,100/mo** ($11/listing)
- White-label team: Platinum Monthly, 30 listings = **$660/mo**

## 14. COMPETITIVE POSITIONING

### Why SnapR wins on price

| What agents pay today (per listing) | SnapR equivalent |
|--------------------------------------|-----------------|
| Real estate photographer: $150-$300 | AI enhancement: included |
| BoxBrownie editing: $30-$100 | Included |
| Virtual staging: $25-$75/room | Included (2/listing on Gold) |
| Social media manager: $500-$2000/mo | Auto-posting: included |
| Video production: $200-$500 | AI video: included |
| MLS description writer: $35-$60 | AI description: included |
| **Total: $350-$1,000+/listing** | **$11-$28/listing** |

### Why competitors can't replicate easily

1. **BoxBrownie** -- Human editors, 24-48hr turnaround. They'd need to rebuild as AI-first. Different DNA.
2. **Virtuance/HomeJab** -- Photography companies with photographers. Can't automate their own workforce away.
3. **Zillow/Redfin** -- Consumer-facing portals. Building agent tools is a conflict with their buyer-lead model.
4. **Canva/Adobe** -- Horizontal tools. Won't build MLS integration, social publishing for 1 vertical.

### SnapR's moat

1. **Switching cost**: Social connections, CRM data, property sites, post history -- none of this migrates
2. **Data flywheel**: Every listing improves AI models (photo classification, description quality)
3. **Network effects**: Property sites generate SEO backlinks -> organic traffic -> more agents
4. **Cost structure**: AI costs are $1.75/listing and falling. Human competitors can't match this at any scale.
5. **Platform breadth**: 23 AI tools + marketing + video + CRM + MLS + social + API in one product

## 15. RISKS & MITIGATIONS

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|-----------|
| Higher churn than modeled | Medium | High | Deep data lock-in (CRM, social, posts). Even at 7% churn, business is viable. |
